# NexCall

NexCall is a messaging and calling app. Messages, photos, voice notes and
calls are end-to-end encrypted: they are locked on the phones, and our server
is not given the keys. Your phone first learns a contact's keys from our
server, so this holds against us only once you and that contact have compared
your safety number. Backups are separate; the privacy policy says more.

- [Privacy policy](privacy): what data NexCall keeps, why, for how long, and
  your rights.
- [Terms of use](terms): the rules for using NexCall.
