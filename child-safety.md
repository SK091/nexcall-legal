# Child safety standards

NexCall has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE),
including child sexual abuse material (CSAM). These are the standards for the
NexCall app, run by {{ site.grievance_name }}.

NexCall is for people aged 18 and over (see the [terms](terms)).

## 1. What is not allowed

- Any sexual content that shows or involves a child (CSAM), real or drawn.
- Grooming a child, or asking a child for sexual content or a sexual meeting.
- Sexual talk with a child, or sexualising a child in any way.
- Threatening or blackmailing a child or anyone else with such content
  (sextortion).
- Sharing, asking for, or trading any of the above.

This is also illegal in India (POCSO Act, 2012; IT Act, 2000; IT Rules, 2021,
Rule 3(1)(b)) and in most countries. Breaking this rule ends your account.

## 2. How to report in the app

You can report a person or one message. You do not need to leave the app.

- **A person:** open the chat, open the contact's info, open the menu and tap
  **Report**.
- **One message you received:** press and hold the message, then tap
  **Report**.
- **A group invitation:** the invitation has a **Report** option.

Choose **Illegal content** as the reason, add a few words, and send. The box
"Also block" is ticked by default. You can also block someone any time from the
same menu.

You can also write to us at the address in section 5.

## 3. What NexCall does with a report

Messages, photos, files and calls are end-to-end encrypted. Our server is not
given the keys, so **we cannot read or see them.** We cannot search for abuse
in your chats.

A report is the one way we learn about a problem:

- It says who you reported, the reason, and the words you wrote.
- If you report one message, your phone also sends us that message's text, who
  sent it and when. Nothing else from the chat is sent.
- A report about a person alone sends no messages.

We read every report. We can mark it new, being reviewed, or resolved, and we
keep notes. When a report shows child sexual abuse material, we suspend the
account at once: it can no longer sign in, send messages, call, or be reached
by anyone. We then report it to the authorities below. Where the law allows,
we keep the report as evidence.


## 4. Reporting to authorities

We report confirmed child sexual abuse material to the authorities.

- **India:** National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal,
  <https://cybercrime.gov.in> (helpline 1930), and your local police.
- **United States and other countries:** the National Center for Missing &
  Exploited Children (NCMEC) CyberTipline,
  <https://report.cybertip.org>, or the police or hotline in your country.

If a child is in danger now, call your local emergency number first. In India,
call 112, or Childline on 1098.

We help police with lawful requests, for the data we actually hold (see the
[terms](terms) and [privacy policy](privacy)).
